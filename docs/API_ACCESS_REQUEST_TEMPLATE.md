# API access request template

Adjust the bracketed sections per recipient. Send from a Somosure company
address, and attach IRA/broker licensing details if Somosure holds them -
insurers and aggregators will want to confirm you're a licensed
intermediary before sharing integration specs.

---

**Subject: API integration inquiry - Somosure Insurance digital platform**

Hi [Name / Partnerships team],

I'm [Maxwell / your name], [role] at Somosure Insurance, a digital
insurance platform based in Nairobi. We're building an insurance
marketplace and quotation engine ([somosure.co.ke] or working URL) that
lets customers compare and buy motor, medical, life, travel, home, and
business covers from multiple underwriters in one place.

We'd like to explore a technical integration with [Company] to:

- Retrieve real-time quotations for [motor / medical / other] products
- Submit applications and supporting documents
- Process policy issuance and retrieve policy documents
- (Where supported) submit and track claims, and handle renewals

Could you point us to the right contact for API/technical integration
discussions, and let us know what's needed on our side (licensing
verification, a signed agency/partner agreement, sandbox access, etc.)?

We're happy to share more about our platform and current provider
integrations on a call.

Thank you,
[Name]
[Somosure Insurance]
[Contact details]

---

## Notes per recipient

- **Lami Technologies** - frame this as an insurance-as-a-service
  integration inquiry; they already position themselves for exactly this
  (banks, platforms, and businesses embedding insurance via their API).
  Mention the specific product lines you want (motor/medical first is a
  reasonable opener) and your expected volume if known - aggregators often
  ask this to scope pricing.
- **mTek / bolttech** - since the December 2025 acquisition, confirm
  whether new integration inquiries route through bolttech's global team
  or mTek's existing Nairobi team; ask directly in your first email.
- **Direct insurers (Britam, Jubilee, APA, CIC, ICEA Lion, Old Mutual,
  Sanlam)** - lead with Somosure's role as a broker/agency if that's
  established, since insurers are more likely to engage in a technical
  conversation with a party they can place a formal agency agreement
  with, rather than a cold API request. If Somosure doesn't yet hold a
  direct agency relationship with the insurer in question, that's
  probably a prerequisite conversation before the API one.
